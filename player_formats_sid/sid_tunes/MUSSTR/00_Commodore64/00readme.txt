This directory contains various Commodore 64 software releated to the
playing and editing of Compute's SidPlayer Music files.

All the software is supplied on D64 disk images.

NOTE: when running the Stereo Sidplayer in the VICE emulator, the Video Standard
needs setting to NTSC to get the player to work properly. You should also consider
enabling the second sid.
